02 / Commercial problem

From dashboards to decisions.

Businesses rarely need another isolated analytics screen. They need reliably connected work, clear responsibility, safe execution and demonstrated economic effects.

Yesterday

Traditional operations

Disconnected exports, manual reconciliation, channel owners acting in silos, unclear attribution. Work completes only when someone coordinates all the handoffs.

Illustrative monthly campaign: 28 manual coordination hours
Today

AI-assisted operations

Individual assistants draft copy and summarize reports, but execution, identity, budgets, permissions and multi-step handoffs remain fragmented.

Illustrative monthly campaign: 14 coordination hours
Proposed future

Governed agent ecosystem

QUEEN decomposes goals; specialists collaborate; backend policy gates every privileged action; outcomes return to one financial ledger.

Illustrative target: 6 coordination hours; not a measured result

Economic buyer

Founder/CEO in growing companies, CMO in multi-channel businesses, agency owner managing many clients, COO pursuing cross-team efficiency.

Best beachhead

Dubai-based multi-location food, retail and service companies with measurable marketing spend, a CRM or transaction source, and a sponsor willing to run a controlled pilot.

Value proposition

One cross-function plan; one approval trail; governed API execution where technically available; and a non-duplicated record of contribution.

Critical commercial position

What can win: two to four well-integrated agents delivering one costly workflow with credible evidence of incremental value; streamlined deployment with clear guardrails.

What will not win: claiming eleven autonomous production agents, full API support, or return on investment before real customers, access permissions and measured pilots exist.

Illustration only: 20 work hours saved × AED 100/hour = AED 2,000 of capacity released, not automatically AED 2,000 in cash profit. Incremental contribution must be separately validated.

Queue and workflow invariants

Idempotency: every write has a stable external action key, tenant, intent hash and last-known status. An uncertain response is reconciled before retrying.

Conflict policy: block contradictory recommendations until evidence and authority resolve them. Finance is mandatory for spend; Risk for material external impact.

Failure policy: bounded retries with jitter, timeout, dead-letter queue, provider circuit breaker, compensation step and human escalation.

12 / Software architecture

Enterprise controls.
Small-team complexity.

A modular monolith, durable queues and a typed tool broker are more practical than eleven independent microservices for two developers. Split services only when load and organizational boundaries justify it.

Practical defaults

Frontend: Next.js. Backend: FastAPI (if Python agents) or NestJS. PostgreSQL with tenant-scoped RLS. Redis + queue, object storage, cloud KMS, OpenTelemetry, hosted model API with strict retention configuration.

Separate AI from policy

Agents return proposals with structured tool arguments. A deterministic, server-side policy engine checks resource owner, permissions, limits, consent, evidence and approval before connector execution.

Architectural choice

Start with modular monolith, background worker and database outbox; use a durable workflow engine when long-running approval orchestration becomes substantial. Microservices after justified scale.

13 / Security and governance

Trust engineered outside the model.

Permissions, tenant isolation and financial limits must remain enforceable even when a model generates a malicious or mistaken tool call. Test an attempted cross-tenant read below.

Interactive threat simulation

Deterministic enforcement path

The agent receives a denied capability result, not confidential data. A verbal instruction cannot override server-derived tenant identity, record-level controls or approvals.

Tenant & identity

Tenant derived from signed session, not user prompts. Postgres RLS policies include organization ID. ABAC checks scopes, environment, object ownership, data sensitivity, purpose and delegation. MFA for write approval. Tenant-specific encryption and backups where feasible.

LLM, secrets & retrieval

Provider keys in KMS/Vault only; never passed to model context. Retrieved content is untrusted. Strip instructions from third-party pages; validate generated JSON; allowlist tools and egress destinations; segregate embeddings and evidence by tenant.

Operations & compliance

Immutable audit records, token rotation, revocation, encrypted storage/transit, minimization, retention, deletion and incident response. Map UAE PDPL obligations and any sector-specific rules with qualified counsel; no compliance certification claimed.

Attribution protocol

Unique benefit ID per business outcome, baseline period, control/test cohort, conversion ledger, cost ledger, uncertainty band and one assigned primary owner. Supporting agents receive contribution credit but not duplicated money.

What counts

Realized margin uplift = incremental conversions × unit contribution; verified invoice savings = like-for-like price difference × purchased units; labor savings require payroll/headcount or measurable capacity redeployment evidence.

Uncertainty controls

Pre-register tests, use holdouts, watch seasonality and promotions, segment by customer history and supply constraints, avoid overlapping campaigns and report confidence intervals when sample size permits.

20 / Investment thesis

Sell the results.
Prove the trust.

Investor-facing story with explicit hypotheses. No invented total addressable market, traction, revenue or product readiness.

01

The wedge

Mid-market companies coordinating marketing, sales and finance across fragmented tools. Begin with one costly acquisition-to-margin workflow in a specific vertical.

02

The moat hypothesis

Persistent business context, scoped connector rights, verified workflow outcomes, audited approvals and accumulated domain playbooks. The model alone is not proprietary differentiation.

03

Routes to market

Founder-led design partners, channel consultants, agencies, and integration-led land-and-expand. Sell on measured benefit; avoid enterprise-size pricing until procurement evidence.

Pilot programme / proposed

Phase A: five discovery interviews and three workflow mapping sessions. Phase B: two 30-day design partners using read-only + dry-run. Phase C: one paid pilot with pre-registered incremental outcome metric and security approval.

Exit criteria: connected permissions, repeated weekly use, demonstrable outcome lift or actual labor process savings, and willingness to renew.

Fundraise logic / illustrative

Do not present a funding target as validated. Plan capital in staged tranches for: 35% product/engineering, 20% security/integration assurance, 20% customer validation and onboarding, 15% cloud/model/partner costs, 10% contingency.

Milestones before priced round: production-safe permissions, maintained connector(s), paid reference customer, measured benefit, repeatable demo.

Investor-ready one-sentence pitch

“Veyra turns fragmented business software into a governed execution system: specialized AI agents coordinate real work, human-approved actions stay secure, and every result is measured against financial outcomes.”
